Privacy

Without an account, no cookies are set. We count use of the site, and each count holds only:

  • the view opened, the referring site’s domain, and your stack’s size
  • the product opened
  • how many products you compare
  • a product added to or removed from your stack, and its new size
  • a short plain-text search, and whether it found anything
  • a share link copied or open data opened
  • the local MCP setup copied or its guide opened
  • page speed
  • your country, browser, operating system and device type, as families
  • a daily count key: a one-way hash of your connection with a random value deleted each day
  • counts of requests to our pages and data files

We never store your IP address, browser string or any identifier that lasts longer than a day. Global Privacy Control and Do Not Track stop your browser sending counts.

Purpose: understanding use and keeping the service working and safe (legitimate interests, GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)).

Accounts

If you sign in, we keep your email address, the ways you sign in (Google, GitHub or a code sent by email), your saved stack, and one cookie that keeps you signed in for up to 30 days. During a Google or GitHub sign-in, a second cookie holds the sign-in check for up to 10 minutes. A sign-in code is stored only as a one-way hash and expires after 10 minutes. When you sign in with Google or GitHub, that service tells us your account identifier, name and verified email address. Sign-in emails are sent through Cloudflare. Purpose: providing the account you asked for (contract, GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)). You can delete your account and saved stack at any time from Account; we keep them until you do.

Retention: count keys, until the day ends; searches fewer than 5 people made, 30 days; other counts, 13 months.

Cloudflare hosts AirStack, stores account data, sends sign-in emails, and processes requests and standard security logs. Cloudflare may process data outside your country, including in the United States; those transfers rely on the EU–US Data Privacy Framework and the Standard Contractual Clauses in Cloudflare’s data processing terms. We do not sell or share personal data.

You may ask for a copy of your data, its correction, deletion or restricted use, or object to its use, and you may complain to your data-protection authority. California residents may ask to know, delete or correct the personal information we hold; using these rights does not change the service you get.
