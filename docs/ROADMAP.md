# Roadmap

Open work, by area. Each item ships only with evidence: a fact needs its quote on its page, and unknown stays unknown.

## Product
- Share a stack by link and export its renewal dates as a calendar file.
- Compare: show each product's own pages side by side.
- Calendar: mark a passed date "occurrence unconfirmed" and keep a delay's history.
- Search: category synonyms, a full results view, and a reviewed query set checked in CI.
- Market stories from typed updates, with rule-based detectors for breakouts and trend changes.
- A makers map sized by measured adoption.

## Evidence
- Independent second sources (app stores, marketplaces, registries); "corroborated" only on an independent match.
- A per-fact evidence receipt, captures fixed by hash, and change history across the corpus.
- Profiles for more products where their own pages are readable.
- List prices for directory products.

## Quality
- Accessibility walk-through to WCAG 2.2 AA, and a mobile performance budget in CI.
- One type scale, two layout breakpoints, and loaders sized to the content they replace.
- More read-only MCP tools, checked in real MCP clients.
